# Support

Do you have a question, found a bug, or need help with Travel Delta? We're happy to assist.

## Contact

Email: [benedikt.schreiner@googlemail.com](mailto:benedikt.schreiner@googlemail.com)

We aim to respond to inquiries within a few business days.

## Common Topics

**Premium not unlocked after purchase**
First, check the app's settings under "Restore Purchases" to see if the premium feature can be unlocked again. If the issue persists, contact us by email with the purchase date and, if available, the receipt from the App Store or Google Play.

**Incorrect or outdated travel information**
Entry requirements, traffic rules, and other travel data can change at short notice. If you spot an error, we'd appreciate a note by email.

**Bugs or crashes**
Please describe as precisely as possible what you were doing when the issue occurred, along with your device model and app version (found in Settings).
